Privacy Policy

Version 1.0, effective [MERGE DATE]

This policy explains what personal data The Tiny Apps Company collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. It follows the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are and what this covers

The Tiny Apps Company ("we", "us") runs this website, thetinyappscompany.com, and the products SpacePartner, ERPBlueprint and TraitAtlas. We are responsible for the personal data we decide how to use, including the data of our customers and of people who use ERPBlueprint and TraitAtlas.

SpacePartner is different: each coworking operator has its own site. The operator decides what data about its members and visitors to keep there, and we process that data on the operator's behalf. If you are a member of a coworking space, the operator's own privacy policy applies to you; write to the operator first.

A product's website may add details specific to that product. They sit alongside this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Contact and account details: your name, email address, phone number, business name and sign-in details.
  • Billing details: the billing name and address, your GSTIN if you give it to us, what you bought, and our invoices.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the payment reference, amount and status, not your full card or bank details.
  • What you put into our products: for ERPBlueprint, your answers about your business and any files you attach; for TraitAtlas, your test answers, scores and any optional "About you" choices; for SpacePartner, the data the operator keeps on its site.
  • Support messages: what you write to us by email, WhatsApp, phone or a help desk.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device details, and logs kept by our hosting providers to run and protect the service.

This website stores only your light or dark theme choice in your browser. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our products use the cookies they need to keep you signed in.

3. Why we use it

  • to provide the product you signed up for or bought, and to support you;
  • to take payments, issue invoices and handle refunds;
  • to keep our products secure and prevent fraud or misuse;
  • to meet legal duties, such as keeping accounting and tax records;
  • to improve our products, using usage information that does not identify you where we can;
  • to send you service messages, and product news only if you have agreed to it. You can opt out of news at any time.

We rely on your consent, on what is needed to give you the service you asked for, and on our legal duties. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what we did before.

4. AI in our products

ERPBlueprint and TraitAtlas send parts of your data to AI providers to write documents and reports. ERPBlueprint reads attached files with AI only if you tick the consent box. TraitAtlas sends only your first name, your scores and any "About you" choices you add; Wellbeing Check-in results are never sent to AI. Each AI provider's own data terms apply to what it receives.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • service providers who run parts of our service for us, such as Razorpay (payments), hosting, email and AI providers, under their data terms;
  • professional advisers, such as our accountants and lawyers, where needed;
  • government or law-enforcement bodies, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside India.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. Invoices and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections, access controls and limited staff access to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • stop using it where you gave consent;
  • let someone you nominate exercise these rights if you die or cannot act.

Write to Apps@thetinyappscompany.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

Our products are meant for adults. If you are under 18, use them only with a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has given us data without that consent, write to us and we will delete it.
