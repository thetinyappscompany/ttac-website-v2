Refund and Cancellation Policy

Version 1.0, effective 12 October 2026

Every payment for SpacePartner, ERPBlueprint and TraitAtlas is made to The Tiny Apps Company, and every refund is made by The Tiny Apps Company, to the account or card you paid from.

1. When we always refund in full

Whatever you bought from us, we refund the full amount if:

  • you were charged twice for the same order;
  • your payment failed or the order did not go through, but money was still taken; or
  • we could not deliver what you paid for, as set out for each product below.

2. ERPBlueprint

No refund is given after a document has been generated. Refunds are made only for a duplicate payment, a failed payment that was still charged, or a broken file we cannot fix within two working days.

3. TraitAtlas

If a report doesn't load or you were charged twice, we refund in full. Because reports are delivered instantly, we don't otherwise refund unlocked reports. Unused team seats can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.

4. SpacePartner

  • Cancelling a subscription: you can cancel your plan or any monthly add-on at any time by writing to us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month or year you have already paid for, and your site keeps working until then. We do not refund part of a period that has started.
  • Setup fee: refunded in full if you cancel before setup work begins. Once we have started setting up your site, the setup fee is not refundable.
  • Support-hour packs: unused hours are refunded on request within 14 days of purchase. After that, or once hours have been used, packs are not refundable. Unused hours expire 12 months after purchase.
  • Custom development: charged only for hours worked, at the agreed rate.
  • Your data: after cancellation, you can ask for an export of your site's data. We will tell you how long we keep it before deleting it.

5. How to ask for a refund or cancel

Write to Apps@thetinyappscompany.com from the email address on your account, with:

  • the product (SpacePartner, ERPBlueprint or TraitAtlas);
  • the order, invoice or Razorpay payment ID; and
  • what went wrong, or what you want to cancel.

6. How long it takes

We reply within 2 working days. Once we approve a refund, we start it through Razorpay within 2 working days, and record it against your order. Your bank or card issuer usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show the money in your account.

7. Chargebacks

Please write to us before raising a chargeback with your bank. Most problems are faster to fix directly.
