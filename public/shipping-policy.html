Shipping and Delivery Policy

Version 1.0, effective 12 October 2026

The Tiny Apps Company sells only digital services, delivered online. We do not ship any physical goods, and there are no shipping charges.

1. How you receive what you buy

Everything is delivered in your account on the product's website, and you can use it from anywhere with an internet connection. After each payment you receive a payment confirmation from Razorpay, and a receipt or invoice from The Tiny Apps Company by email.

ERPBlueprint

After you pay, your document is generated in your ERPBlueprint account at erpblueprint.io, where you can view and download it. If it does not appear, or the file is broken, write to us. If we cannot fix it within two working days, we refund you in full.

TraitAtlas

Reports are unlocked in your TraitAtlas account at traitatlas.in as soon as your payment succeeds, with a PDF to download. Pack credits and team seats are added to your account at once, and stay valid for 12 months.

SpacePartner

SpacePartner runs on a dedicated site for each coworking operator. After the setup fee is paid, we set up your site, import your data and train your team, on the timeline agreed in the proposal you accepted. Sign-in details are sent to the email address on your order. Monthly and yearly plans give continued access to your site for the period paid for.

2. Where we deliver

Our products are offered to customers in India and are available online wherever you can reach the product's website.

3. If something has not arrived

Write to Apps@thetinyappscompany.com with your order, invoice or Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 2 working days. If we cannot deliver what you paid for, we refund you as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
